Frequently Asked Questions

We want you to feel completely comfortable before you begin.

Do I need to be flexible or sit on the floor?
Not at all! Our sessions are specifically designed for seniors. We use comfortable, sturdy chairs, and all movements are gentle and adaptable to your body's needs. No flexibility is required.
What should I wear or bring to my first session?
Just wear whatever makes you feel comfortable and relaxed. We provide all the materials, water, and a warm environment. Just bring yourself and an open mind!
Can I change or cancel my plan later?
Absolutely. There are no long-term contracts. You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel your plan at any time by simply speaking with our front desk staff or giving us a call.
I have never meditated before. Is that okay?
It is more than okay—it is wonderful! Our Starter plan is built exactly for beginners. Our instructors will guide you through every single breath, ensuring you never feel lost or overwhelmed.
Do you offer any discounts for couples or spouses?
Yes! If you and a spouse or partner sign up together, we offer a 15% "Companion Discount" on your monthly plans. Just let us know when you contact us to sign up.

Still Have Questions?

Our friendly team is always happy to chat. Give us a call or send a message, and we'll help you find the perfect fit.
