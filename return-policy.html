Return & Refund Policy

Our commitment to fair, transparent, and compassionate cancellation and refund practices.

1. General Policy

Because Life and Peace provides time-based wellness services and reserved spots in our meditation sessions, our "Return Policy" functions as a Cancellation and Refund Policy. We strive to be as flexible and understanding as possible, while maintaining the sustainability of our studio and our instructors.

2. Cancellation of Monthly Plans

All of our meditation plans (Starter, Pro, and Max) operate on a month-to-month basis. There are no long-term contracts. You may cancel your plan at any time.

To avoid being charged for the following month, you must cancel your subscription at least 48 hours before your next billing date. Once cancelled, you will retain access to your plan until the end of your current billing cycle.

3. Refund Eligibility

First-Time Members (7-Day Guarantee)

We want you to feel completely comfortable with your decision to join us. If you are a brand new member and feel that our services are not the right fit for you, we offer a 7-day money-back guarantee. If you cancel within the first 7 days of your very first billing cycle, we will issue a full refund, no questions asked.

Standard Refunds

After the initial 7-day period, monthly plan fees are generally non-refundable. Because we reserve a specific spot for you in our classes and allocate instructor time, we cannot provide pro-rated refunds for the remainder of a month if you choose to cancel mid-cycle. Your access will simply continue until the end of the paid period.

4. Missed Sessions and "No-Shows"

We understand that life happens, especially as we get older. However, because our class sizes are intentionally kept small to ensure personal attention, we cannot roll over unused days to the next month, nor can we provide refunds for sessions that are missed or cancelled on the day of the class.

If you know you will be absent, we kindly ask that you notify our front desk at least 12 hours in advance so we can offer your spot to someone on our waitlist.

5. Medical Emergencies and Compassionate Clause

The health and well-being of our seniors is our absolute top priority. If you are forced to miss sessions or must cancel your plan entirely due to a sudden illness, hospitalization, or medical emergency, we offer a Compassionate Medical Clause.

In such events, please email us with a brief note (and a doctor's note if available). We will gladly pause your membership at no cost, or provide a pro-rated refund for the unused portion of your current month, ensuring you are not financially penalized for a health issue.

6. How to Request a Cancellation or Refund

To cancel your plan or request a refund under our 7-day guarantee or Medical Clause, please send us an email. We aim to process all cancellation requests and approved refunds within 3 to 5 business days. Refunds will be issued to the original method of payment.
