Welcome to Life and Peace

At Life and Peace, we believe that inner calm has no age limit. Based in Amarillo, Texas, we specialize in creating a safe, gentle, and nurturing environment where seniors can explore the transformative power of meditation. Our certified instructors understand the unique needs of older adults, offering patient guidance every step of the way.

Why Choose Life and Peace?

Certified Instructors

Our teachers are specially trained in senior-focused meditation techniques, ensuring a safe and effective experience.

Gentle Pace

We move at your speed. No rushing, no pressure—just calm, mindful guidance tailored to your comfort level.

Senior-Focused

Every session is designed with elderly practitioners in mind, addressing common concerns like mobility and focus.
