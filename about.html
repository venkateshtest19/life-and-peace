About Life and Peace

Rooted in Amarillo, dedicated to the well-being of our senior community. Discover the story behind our gentle approach to meditation.

Our Story

Life and Peace was founded with a simple, heartfelt belief: inner calm has no age limit. As we watched our parents and grandparents navigate the challenges of aging—dealing with stress, sleepless nights, and the feeling of being left behind—we realized that traditional meditation classes weren't built for them. They moved too fast, sat on the floor, and didn't address the unique physical and emotional needs of older adults.

So, we created Life and Peace. Located right here in the heart of Amarillo at 4615 S Virginia St, we have built a sanctuary specifically for seniors. Our space is warm, accessible, and entirely judgment-free. Here, meditation isn't about perfect posture or clearing your mind completely; it's about finding moments of joy, reducing physical tension, and connecting with a wonderful community of peers.

Our Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To empower older adults to live healthier, happier, and more peaceful lives by providing accessible, gentle, and expertly guided meditation programs. We strive to create a safe harbor where seniors can relieve stress, improve their cognitive and physical well-being, and rediscover the joy of the present moment.

Our Vision

We envision a community in Amarillo—and beyond—where aging is embraced with grace and tranquility. We see a future where every senior has access to the tools they need to maintain their mental sharpness, emotional balance, and physical comfort, proving that the golden years can truly be the most peaceful years.

Our Gentle Approach

We don't believe in a "one size fits all" method. Every session is carefully crafted for the elderly body and mind.

Chair-Friendly Meditation

No need to sit cross-legged on the floor. All our sessions can be done comfortably in a sturdy chair, ensuring zero strain on your joints, back, or knees while you relax.

Breathwork for Vitality

We teach simple, safe breathing techniques that help lower blood pressure, improve lung capacity, and instantly calm the nervous system when anxiety arises.

Memory & Focus Exercises

Our guided visualizations and mindfulness practices are designed to gently stimulate the brain, helping to maintain cognitive sharpness and improve daily focus.

Patient, Certified Guidance

Our instructors are not just certified; they are deeply compassionate. They speak clearly, move slowly, and are always available to answer questions or offer a reassuring hand.

Our Core Values

The principles that guide every interaction, every class, and every breath we take together.

  • Compassion First We treat every member like family. We listen with empathy, understand your unique struggles, and celebrate your small victories.
  • Unwavering Patience Learning a new skill at any age takes time. We never rush. We honor your pace and ensure you never feel pressured or left behind.
  • Community & Connection Loneliness is a real challenge for seniors. We foster a warm, welcoming environment where lifelong friendships are naturally formed.
  • Integrity & Trust We are transparent about our methods, our pricing, and our goals. Your safety and trust are the foundations of our practice.
